# SWOT

Source: [Loop Engineering Methodology Skill Generator](https://loopengineering.app/methodology-skill-generator/)

## Definition

Compare strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats before choosing a
direction.

## Fit

- Best when a decision, project, market, or product direction needs a balanced
  strategic view.
- Avoid when one operational failure needs root-cause analysis.

## Principles

- Keep internal factors separate from external factors.
- Do not turn the analysis into a wish list.
- Connect observations to decisions.
- Name the resulting strategic move.

## Steps

1. List internal strengths.
2. List internal weaknesses.
3. List external opportunities.
4. List external threats.
5. Recommend one move supported by the analysis.

## Quality questions

- Are strengths and weaknesses internal?
- Are opportunities and threats external?
- Does the recommendation follow from the analysis?
- Are uncertainties visible?

## Stop

Stop after one evidence-backed direction is stated. Route a single operational
failure to root-cause analysis instead. Stop and ask for human help if the task
does not fit SWOT, required context is missing, or validation cannot be
satisfied without guessing.
