# AIDA

Source: [Loop Engineering Methodology Skill Generator](https://loopengineering.app/methodology-skill-generator/)

## Definition

Structure persuasive copy through attention, interest, desire, and action.

## Fit

- Best when a page, post, or email should move a reader toward one action.
- Avoid when the content must remain purely instructional or neutral.

## Principles

- Earn attention with a concrete problem.
- Build interest with useful detail.
- Create desire with a credible outcome.
- Ask for one clear action.

## Steps

1. Write the attention line.
2. Explain why the problem matters.
3. Show the outcome the reader wants.
4. Give one next action.

## Quality questions

- Does the opening name a real problem?
- Is the benefit credible?
- Is there only one main action?
- Does the copy avoid hype?

## Stop

Stop after one clear action. Use a neutral explanatory method when persuasion
is not appropriate. Stop and ask for human help if the task does not fit AIDA,
required context is missing, or validation cannot be satisfied without
guessing.
