# Pyramid Principle

Source: [Loop Engineering Methodology Skill Generator](https://loopengineering.app/methodology-skill-generator/)

## Definition

Lead with the answer, then arrange supporting points so each lower level
explains the level above it.

## Fit

- Best when a recommendation, memo, update, or executive summary needs a clear
  conclusion.
- Avoid when the work is exploratory and no defensible conclusion exists yet.

## Principles

- State the conclusion first.
- Group supporting points using one consistent logic.
- Keep groups mutually distinct.
- Order points by importance, time, or structure.

## Steps

1. Write the one-sentence answer.
2. List three to five supporting arguments.
3. Confirm that each argument supports the answer directly.
4. Remove overlaps and reorder the arguments.
5. Present the conclusion at the top.

## Quality questions

- Is the first sentence a real conclusion?
- Does every argument support it?
- Are the groups non-overlapping?
- Can a busy reader understand the answer without reading every detail?

## Stop

Stop after the conclusion and its support are reviewable. Stop and ask for
human help if the task does not fit Pyramid Principle, required context is
missing, or validation cannot be satisfied without guessing.
