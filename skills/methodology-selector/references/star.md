# STAR

Source: [Loop Engineering Methodology Skill Generator](https://loopengineering.app/methodology-skill-generator/)

## Definition

Explain a real example through its situation, task, action, and result.

## Fit

- Best when a concise case study, interview answer, or project story is needed.
- Avoid when the work needs an argument or recommendation rather than a story.

## Principles

- Begin with the relevant context.
- State the responsibility.
- Show the action taken.
- End with a concrete result.

## Steps

1. Describe the situation.
2. Clarify the task or responsibility.
3. Explain the actions.
4. State the result and evidence.
5. Add one useful lesson when applicable.

## Quality questions

- Is every STAR element present?
- Is the action specific?
- Is the result measurable or observable?
- Is unnecessary background removed?

## Stop

Stop when the result closes the example. Choose an argument method when the
task is to recommend rather than narrate. Stop and ask for human help if the
task does not fit STAR, required context is missing, or validation cannot be
satisfied without guessing.
