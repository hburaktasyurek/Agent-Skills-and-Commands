# Feynman Technique

Source: [Loop Engineering Methodology Skill Generator](https://loopengineering.app/methodology-skill-generator/)

## Definition

Explain an idea in plain language, expose gaps, simplify again, and test the
explanation with an example.

## Fit

- Best when a concept must be simple enough to reveal understanding gaps.
- Avoid when the audience already requires advanced technical detail.

## Principles

- Prefer plain words.
- Expose gaps instead of hiding them.
- Use concrete examples.
- Repeat until the explanation becomes clearer.

## Steps

1. Explain the idea to a beginner.
2. Mark confusing or vague parts.
3. Return to the source or an example.
4. Rewrite in simpler language.
5. Test the explanation with a concrete case.

## Quality questions

- Could a beginner follow it?
- Are vague terms explained?
- Is there a concrete example?
- Did the explanation become shorter or clearer?

## Stop

Stop when the explanation is both accurate and plain. Preserve necessary
technical detail when simplification would make it false. Stop and ask for
human help if the task does not fit Feynman Technique, required context is
missing, or validation cannot be satisfied without guessing.
