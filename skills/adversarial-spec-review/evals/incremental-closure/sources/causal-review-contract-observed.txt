Observed review-output failure, sanitized

Review reports can stop at a visible symptom and then prescribe a local patch.
That spends the stronger review model on implementation design while leaving
the implementer without a stable causal contract. A useful finding instead
owns the deepest task-owned root cause, explains why the current mechanism or
proof model is incapable, maps the consequence surface, states a falsifiable
outcome-level closure condition, names independent proof obligations, and says
whether the artifact needs revision, implementation, or additional evidence.
Exact code, tool, parser, algorithm, or file-edit recommendations are not part
of the default finding unless binding authority leaves no alternative.
